# Privacy Policy for UniConnect

Last updated: October 01, 2026

**Uniquode Developers** built the **UniConnect** mobile application as a Free contact management service. This SERVICE is provided by Uniquode Developers at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone deciding to use our Service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:
- Full Name
- Email Address
- User-submitted inquiry details and notes

The information that you submit will be retained and used solely to process, manage, and respond to your direct service inquiries.

### 2. Data Protection and Security
We value your trust in providing us your personal information. We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing services. All transmitted information is handled with standard secure encryption protocols.

### 3. Log Data and Analytics
UniConnect does not collect background telemetry or analytics trackers without your consent. Standard device diagnostics may be recorded temporarily for application stability and crash debugging.

### 4. Third-Party Services
UniConnect does not utilize third-party advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our records.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
